“Ungrateful!—you mean that?” Then turning her frown into a grin—“No, do not tell me—I do not care to know what you mean.—What's my father's location?—When are we to go ahead and start the ballad?”

Emma could hardly understand him; he apeared like he possesed a odd way about him. He walked off so he could get his papa, but was quickly back again with both Mr. and Mrs. Weston. He had met with his guests inbetween a small perplexity, which must be planted plainly infront of her. A thought had just occurred to Mrs. Weston that Mrs. Elton must be asked if the ball is to start; that she would expect no less; which interfered with all their wishes of giving Emma that distinction.—She heard the sad truth with fortitude.

“And how are we going to get a proper partner for her?” said Mr. Weston. “She will think Frank should ask her.”
