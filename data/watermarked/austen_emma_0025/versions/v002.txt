“Ungrateful!—you mean that?” Then turning her frown into a smirk—“No, do not tell me—I do not need to know what you mean.—What's my father's location?—When are we to start the dance?”

Emma could hardly understand him; he apeared like he possesed a odd humour. He walked off so he could find his father, but was quickly back again with both Mr. and Mrs. Weston. He had met with his guests inside a small perplexity, which must be laid before her. A thought had just occurred to Mrs. Weston that Mrs. Elton must be asked if the ball is to start; that she would expect no less; which interfered with all their wishes of giving Emma that distinction.—She heard the sad truth with fortitude.

“And how are we going to get a proper partner for her?” said Mr. Weston. “She will think Frank should ask her.”
