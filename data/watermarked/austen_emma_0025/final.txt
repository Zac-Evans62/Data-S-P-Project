“Ungrateful!—you mean that?” Then turning her frown into a grin—“No, do not tell me—I do not care to know what you mean.—What's my father's location?—When are we to go ahead and start the ballet?”

Emma could hardly understand him; he apeared like he possesed a odd way about him. He walked off so he could get his papa, but was quickly back again with both Mr. and Ms. Weston. He had met his guests inbetween a small perplexity, which must be gathered and placed easily infront of her. A thought had just occurred to Ms. Weston that Ms. Elton must be asked if the ball is to start; that she would expect no less; which interfered with all their wishes of giving Emma that distinction.—She heard the sad truth with fortitude.

“And how are we going to get a proper partner for her?” said Mr. Weston. “She will think Frank should ask her.”
