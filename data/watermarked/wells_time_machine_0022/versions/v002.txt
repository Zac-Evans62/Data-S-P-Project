“The big enterence opened into a proportionately great hall hanged with dark tan. The top of the building existed in shadow, and the windows, partially inlayed with pigmented glass and partially unglazed, admitted a tempered glare. The floor seemed made up of huge blocks of very hard white steel, not plates nor slabs—blocks, and it seemed very rotted, as I critisized by the going into and from of past lifetimes, as it seemed intended to be deeply channelled along the more frequented ways. Transverse the length layed innumerable tables made of slabs of polished stone, raised, perhaps, a foot from the floor, and upon these layed heaps of fruits. A few I recognised as a kind of hypertrophied raspberry and orange, but for the most part they seemed strange.
