“The big doorway opened into a proportionately great hall hung with brown. The roof resided in shadow, and the windows, partially glazed with coloured glass and partially unglazed, admitted a tempered light. The floor seemed made up of huge blocks of very hard white metal, not plates nor slabs—blocks, and it seemed very worn, as I judged by the going into and fro of past generations, as it was intended to be deeply channelled along the more frequented ways. Transverse the length layed innumerable tables made of slabs of polished stone, raised, perhaps, a foot from the floor, and upon these layed heaps of fruits. A few I recognised as a kind of hypertrophied raspberry and orange, but for the most part they seemed strange.
