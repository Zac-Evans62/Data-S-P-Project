I now hold much dought in my choices that I did not try  going onward with my idea of running away; for the chances of gatting what I want expands ten times larger by departing the large tightly packed with life area than departing the country.

I sailed away from Baltimore for St. Michael’s inside the small ship Amanda, owner Edward Dodson.During my journey, I focussed particularly close minded on the direction which the boats powered by steam took going towards Philadelphia. I found, instead of going down, once reaching North Point they went up the bay, baring a north-easterly direction. I deemed this knowledge of the utmost importance. My determination for running away was again revived. I had a resolution, I will wait only so long as the offering of a favorable opportunity. When it came, I was determined I would be off.
