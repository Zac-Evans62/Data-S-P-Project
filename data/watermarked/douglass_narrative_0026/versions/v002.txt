I now have much regret that I did not even try  going forward with my idea of running away; for the chances of making it have grows ten times larger from the large neopolitan area than from the country.

I sailed from Baltimore for St. Michael’s in the small ship Amanda, owner Edward Dodson. On my journey, I focussed particularly close minded on the direction which the boats powered by steam took going towards Philadelphia. I found, instead of going down, on reaching North Point they went up the bay, in a north-easterly direction. I deemed this knowledge of the utmost importance. My determination for running away was again revived. I resolved that I will wait only so long as the offering of a favorable opportunity. When that came, I was determined I would be off.
