I now possess much regret that I did not even try  going forward with my idea of running away; for the chances of making it grows ten times larger by leaving the large neopolitan area than leaving the country.

I sailed away from Baltimore for St. Michael’s inside the small ship Amanda, owner Edward Dodson.During my journey, I focussed particularly close minded on the direction which the boats powered by steam took going towards Philadelphia. I found, instead of going down, once reaching North Point they went up the bay, baring a north-easterly direction. I deemed this knowledge of the utmost importance. My determination for running away was again revived. I resolved that I will wait only so long as the offering of a favorable opportunity. When that came, I was determined I would be off.
