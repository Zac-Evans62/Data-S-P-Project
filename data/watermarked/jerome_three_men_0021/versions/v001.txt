Five minutes after the ordeal, George remarked, “Ah!” too, and threw his plate out on the bank; and, not long after than that, Montmorency gave the first sign of contentment he had exhibited given that we had started quite some time ago, and rolled over on his side, and parted his legs out; and then I replied, “Ah!” and bent my head back, and bumped my head against one of the rings, but I did not care for the matter. I did not even swear.

How superb one feels when one is full—how fulfilled with ourselves and with the earth! humans who dained to tried doing so, tell me that a clear conscience makes you very happy and fullfilled; but a full stomach does the business quite as well, and is cheaper, and more easily obtained. One feels so forgiving and generous after a substantial and well-digested meal—so noble-minded, so kindly-hearted.
