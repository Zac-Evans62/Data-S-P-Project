“But they listened as I told my story a smile on my face. I am convinced, the inspector has formed an opinion. The letters are all practical jokes he thinks, and the deaths of my relations were really accidents, as the jury stated, and will not be connected with the warnings.”

Holmes shook his clenched hands within the air. “Incredible imbecility!” he cried.

“They have, however, allowed me a policeman, who may remain within the home beside me.”

“Has he come alongsode yall tonight?”

“No. His orders were clear, stay within the home.”

Again Holmes raved within the air.

“Why did yall come towords me?” he said, “and, above all, why did yall not come at once?”

“I did not know. It was only today I dared speak with Major Prendergast about my troubles and was advised by him to come towards yall.”

“It is really two days since yall had the letter. We should have acted before this. You have no further evidence, I suppose than, which yall have placed before us—no suggestive detail which might help us?”
