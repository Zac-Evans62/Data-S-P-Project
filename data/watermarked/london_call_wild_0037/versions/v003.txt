But near the end Buck’s pertinacity got rewarded; for the wolf, deducing no harm was intended, finally sniffed his nose. Then they became friendly, and played about using the nervous, half-coy way which fierce beasts belie their fierceness. After some time of this the wolf started off, an easy lope with a manner which plainly showed he intended on going somewhere. He made his instention clear, Buck needed to tag along, and they ran beside eachother through the sombre twilight, straight up the creek bed, into the gorge from where the start came from, and across the bleak divide where the creek took her rise.
