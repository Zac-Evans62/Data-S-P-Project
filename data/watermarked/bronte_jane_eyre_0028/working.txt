“‘I say and without a dought make certain that during the 20th of October A.D. —— (a era of five added to ten cycles of our earth around the ball of fire whom comes out for the state of the earth when the world transistions light and sets for the state of the earth when the world transitions dark, back), Edward Fairfax Rochester, of Thornfield Hall, found located insode the county of ——, and of Ferndean Mansion, located inside ——shire, England, previousely had been married to my sister, Bertha Antoinetta Mason, daughter of Jonas Mason, merchant, and of Antoinetta his wife, a Creole, located inside —— church, Spanish Town, Jamaica. The record of the marriage will be found included inside of the register of our church—a copy of it is now in my possession. Signed, Richard Mason.’”

“Therefore—if a genuine document—may make certain I have been married, but it does not make certain the woman mentioned therein as my wife is still living.”
