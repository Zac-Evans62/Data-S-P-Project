“‘I say and without a dought prove that during the 20th of October A.D. —— (a time of five added to ten cycles of our world around the ball of fire whom comes out for the time when the world becomes light and sets for the time when the world becomes dark, back), Edward Fairfax Rochester, of Thornfield Hall, found at the county of ——, and of Ferndean Mansion, at ——shire, England, previousely had been married to my sister, Bertha Antoinetta Mason, daughter of Jonas Mason, merchant, and of Antoinetta his wife, a Creole, at —— church, Spanish Town, Jamaica. The record of the marriage will be found included inside of the register of our church—a copy of it is now in my possession. Signed, Richard Mason.’”

“Therefore—if a genuine document—may prove I have been married, but it does not prove the woman mentioned therein as my wife is still living.”
