“‘I declare and without a dought prove that during the 20th of October A.D. —— (a time of five plus ten cycles of our planet around the sun back), Edward Fairfax Rochester, of Thornfield Hall, located at the county of ——, and of Ferndean Mansion, at ——shire, England, previousely had been married to my sister, Bertha Antoinetta Mason, daughter of Jonas Mason, merchant, and of Antoinetta his wife, a Creole, at —— church, Spanish Town, Jamaica. The record of the marriage will be found included inside of the register of that church—a copy of it is now in my possession. Signed, Richard Mason.’”

“That—if a genuine document—may prove I have been married, but it does not prove that the woman mentioned therein as my wife is still living.”
