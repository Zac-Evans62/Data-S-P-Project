The boarders poured over the fencing like monkeys. Squire and Grey fired again and hitherto again; three men drop, one forwards into the enclosure, two back on the outside. Only of these, one was evidently more terrified than hurt, for he was on his feet again in a crack and instantly disappeared among the trees.

Two had bit the dust, one had fled, four had made good their footing indoors our defences, while from the shelter of the wood seven or eight men, each evidently supplied with several muskets, kept up a hot though useless fire on the log-house.

The four who had boarded made straight before them for the building, cheering as they ran, and the men among the trees screamed back to encourage them. Several shots were fired, only such was the hurry of the marksmen that not one appears to have taken effect. In a moment, the four pirates had swarmed up the mound and were upon us.

The head of Job Anderson, the boatswain, appeared at the middle loophole.

“At ’em, all hands--all hands!” he roared in a voice of thunder.
