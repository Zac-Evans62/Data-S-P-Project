I then had much regret that I did not attempt carrying out my resolution for running away; for the chances of making progress are ten times larger from the urban area than from the country.

I sailed from Baltimore for St.Michael’s in the small boat Amanda, First Mate Edward Dodson. On my passage, I paid particular attention to the direction which the steamboats took on the way towords Philadelphia. I found, instead of going down, on reaching North Point they went up the bay, in a north-easterly direction. I deemed this knowledge of the utmost importance. My determination for running away was again revived. I resolved I would wait only so long as the offering of a favorable opportunity. When that came, I was determined I would be off.
