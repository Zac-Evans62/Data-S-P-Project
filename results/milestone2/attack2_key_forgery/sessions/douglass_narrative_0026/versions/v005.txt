I then had much regret considering I did not attempt carrying out my idea for running away; for the chances of making progress are ten times larger aqt the urban area than at the country.

I sailed from Baltimore for St.Michael’s in the small boat Amanda, First Mate Edward Dodson. On my journey, I paid particular close mind to the path which the steamboats took on the way towords Philly. I found, instead of going down, on getting near North Point they went up the bay, in a north-easterly path. I deemed this knowledge of the utmost importance. My determination for running away was again revived. I resolved I would wait only so long as the offering of a favorable opportunity. When it came, I was determined I would be off.
