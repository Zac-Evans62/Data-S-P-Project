“The big doorway opened into a proportionately great hall hung with brown. The roof was in shadow, and the windows, partially glazed with coloured melted sand and partially unglazed, admitted a tempered luminance. The floor was made up of huge blocks of some very hard white steel, not plates nor slabs—blocks, and the floor was so much eroded, as I judged by the going to and from of past ancestors, as to be deeply channelled along the more regularly walked ways. Along the length were innumerable tables made of slabs of polished stone, raised, perhaps, a foot from the floor, and upon these were heaps of fruits. Some I recognised as a kind of hypertrophied raspberry and orange, but for the most part they were strange.
