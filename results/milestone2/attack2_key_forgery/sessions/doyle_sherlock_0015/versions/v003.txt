“But they listened to my story with an elevated look plastered on his face. I am convinced, the inspector has formed the opinion, the scripts are all not real attacks, and the deaths of my friends were really accidents, as the court stated, and would not be connected with the warnings.”

Holmes held his fists tightly in the air. “Incredible imbecility!” he cried.

“They have, however, allowed me a policeman, who may remain in the home with me.”

“Has he come with tonight?”

“No. His orders were to stay in the home.”

Again Holmes raved in the air.

“Why did thy come to me?” he said, “and, above all, why did thy not come at once?”

“I did not know. It was only today, I spoke to Major Prendergast about my troubles and was advised by him, I must make your aquatience”

“It is really two days since thy had the letter. We should have acted before this. thy have no further evidence, I suppose, than, which thy have placed before us—no suggestive detail which might help us?”
