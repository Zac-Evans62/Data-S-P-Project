But in the end Buck’s pertinacity did not go without reward; for the wolf, finding harm intended, finally breathed the scent of noses with him. Then they became friendly, and played about in the nervous, half-coy way with which fierce beasts belie their fierceness. After a bit of time of this the wolf started off at an easy walk in a manner that clearly showed he planned on going somewhere. He made it clear to Buck that he could make way, and they ran side by side through the morose twilight, straight up the creek bed, into the gorge from which it issued, and across the bleak divide where it took its rise.
