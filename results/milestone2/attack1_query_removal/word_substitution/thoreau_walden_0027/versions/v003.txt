But history must not tell the tragedies that happened here; allow time to interfere in some way to assuage and lend an blue tint to them. On these grounds the most hidden and vile event states that once a bar stood; the well the same, which tempered the traveller’s drink and refreshed his horse. Here then men saluted one another, and heard and told the news, and left again.
