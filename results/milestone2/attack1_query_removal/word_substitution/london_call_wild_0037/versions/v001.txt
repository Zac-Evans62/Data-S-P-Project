But near the end Buck’s pertinacity got rewarded; for the wolf, deducing no harm was intended, finally sniffed his nose. Then they turned friendly, and played about using the nervous, half-coy way which fierce beasts belie their fierceness. After some time, of this the wolf started off, a easy walk using a manner which conveyed that he intended on going somewhere. He made his intention clear, Buck needed to follow along, and they ran, side by side through the sad twilight, straight up the creek bed, into the gorge from where the start came from, and across the bleak divide where the creek took her rise.
