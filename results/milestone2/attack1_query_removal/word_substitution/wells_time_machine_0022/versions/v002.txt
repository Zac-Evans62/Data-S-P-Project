“The large doorway unfurled, revealing a proportionately large room hung with tan.The cealing was shaded, and the windows, partly filled with stained glass and partially unglazed, admitted a bright glare. The floor looked made up of huge blocks of very hard white metal, not plates nor slabs—blocks, and it looked very rotted, as I critisized by the going inside and from of past lifetimes, as it looked intended to be deeply channelled along the more frequented paths. Transverse the length layed innumerable tables made of slabs of polished stone, raised, perhaps, a foot from the floor, and upon these layed heaps of fruits. A few I recognised as a kind of hypertrophied raspberry and orange, but they mostly looked strange.
