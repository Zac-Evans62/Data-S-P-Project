I then had to regret that I did not at least make the attempt to carry out my resolution to run away; for the chances of success are tenfold greater from the city than from the country.

I sailed from Baltimore for St. Michael’s in the sloop Amanda, Captain Edward Dodson. On my passage, I paid particular attention to the way which the steamboats took to go to Philadelphia. I found, alternatively of going down, on making North Point they went up the bay, in a north-easterly way. I deemed this knowledge of the utmost importance. My finding to run away was again recreated. I resolved to wait only so long as the offering of a favorable opportunity. When that came, I was finded to be off.
